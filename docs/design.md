# LifeOS — Design Notes

## Theme
Premium dark UI. Background near-black (#0B0D12) with warm dawn-orange →
gold accent gradient, evoking the 🌄 sunrise mark.

## Signature element
The **sunrise arc** — a half-arc progress ring — appears wherever something
is trending toward a goal: Daily Standards score, Finance goal %, Fitness
goal %. It's the one visual idea repeated across every page.

## Typography
- Display: Fraunces (headings, quotes)
- Body: Space Grotesk (UI text)
- Mono: JetBrains Mono (numbers, scores, money)
